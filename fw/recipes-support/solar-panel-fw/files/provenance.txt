Provenance of panel-mipi-dbi-spi.bin
=====================================

Purpose: NV3007 display-controller init sequence for the TZT 2.79" 142x428
IPS module, encoded as the firmware blob the kernel panel-mipi-dbi driver
requests by compatible name (see drivers/gpu/drm/tiny/panel-mipi-dbi.c,
kernel 6.18.33: magic "MIPI DBI" + 7 NUL, u8 version 1, then
command/num_params/params records; NOP+1 param = delay in ms).

Generated (do not edit the .bin by hand) with:

    python3 fw/tools/lvgl-nv3007-to-mipi-dbi.py \
        --src inv_ctl/build/_deps/lvgl-src/src/drivers/display/nv3007/lv_nv3007.c \
        -o fw/recipes-support/solar-panel-fw/files/panel-mipi-dbi-spi.bin

Source: LVGL v9.4.0 (tag c016f72d4c125098287be5e83c0f1abed4706ee5, same
SRCREV as meta-oe's lvgl recipe), src/drivers/display/nv3007/lv_nv3007.c,
whose init list is itself "based on ArduinoGFX NV3007 driver".
Note: LVGL delays are in 10 ms units (lv_lcd_generic_mipi_send_cmd_list),
so the post-SLPOUT delay converts to 220 ms.
